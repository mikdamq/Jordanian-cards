## Who we sound like

The cousin who runs every family sahra: warm, quick, a little cheeky, and everyone feels included.

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| **Warm**, like a majlis welcome | Formal, corporate, Modern Standard Arabic |
| **Cheeky**, teasing but never mean | Sarcastic, rude, or punching down |
| **Proud** of Jordan and Arab culture | Preachy, nationalistic or exclusive |
| **Quick**, with short lines and punchlines | Long explanations |
| **Inclusive**, so jiddo and the kids both get it | Insider-only slang that shuts people out |

## Writing Jordanian

Write the way people type it on WhatsApp in Amman, with simple consistent spelling.

**Our spellings**

| Write | Not | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| هسّا | هلأ، الآن | now |
| شو | ماذا | what |
| بدّك / بدكم | تريد | you want |
| كتير | كثيراً | a lot |
| إشي | شيء | something |
| هيك | هكذا | like this |
| عنجد | حقاً | seriously |
| يلا | هيا | come on |
| زلمة | رجل | man, dude |
| منيح | جيد | good |

**Rules**
- Use shadda (ّ) where it changes the sound: «مثّلها»، «ولّعها». Skip other diacritics.
- Use the Arabic ellipsis «…» before a punchline.
- Use Eastern Arabic numerals «٣ جولات» in Arabic copy and Western numerals in Arabizi and English.
- Store and partner copy: keep it Jordanian-flavoured but drop the heaviest slang (زلمة، عنجد) so every Arab reads it easily.

## The Arabizi option

Every key message has **three versions**: Arabic, Arabizi and English. Arabizi is for diaspora kids, Gen Z, and anyone who reads Latin letters faster.

**Our Arabizi conventions**

| Sound | Write | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ع | 3 | 3ala, 3eleh |
| ح | 7 | 7aki, sa7 |
| ء / urban ق | 2 | 2alb, sa2a |
| خ | kh | khalas |
| غ | gh | ghali |
| ش | sh | shoo |
| long vowels | ee / oo / a | kteer, shoo, yalla |

- Spell as people speak, not as the word is written in Arabic: «هسّا» → *hassa*, «بدّك» → *biddak*.
- Matches the card Arabizi already in the app.

**Three-version examples**

| Arabic | Arabizi | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «مثّلها… بس ولا كلمة!» | *Massilha… bas wala kelmeh!* | *Act it out. Not a word.* |
| «ولّعها… بس بدون حكي!» | *Walla3ha… bas bdoon 7aki!* | *Light it up. No talking.* |
| «السهرة بتبلّش هون» | *Il sahra btballish hon* | *The sahra starts here.* |
| «جاهزين؟» | *Jahzeen?* | *Ready?* |

**Where Arabizi shows up**
- Social: an Arabizi line under the Arabic caption on diaspora and Gen Z posts
- Cards: already on every card
- Product idea (not built): an **Arabizi interface mode** in settings, so the whole UI can be read in Latin letters

## Emoji and mixing

Playful is the default. Use **1 to 3 emoji per caption**, at the end of a line or as bullets, never mid-word.

**Brand emoji:** 🤫 (our gesture) 🎭 🔥 😂 🏆 ⏱️ 🥁 🇯🇴

**Use carefully:** 🇯🇴 for Jordan-specific moments, not on every regional post.

Mixing English words like *yalla*, *sahra* and *challenge* into Arabic captions is fine, and so is the reverse. The one rule: **one language leads per line**.

## The formula: «___ها… بس ولا كلمة!»

A verb with «ها», an ellipsis, then «بس ولا كلمة!» (or «بس بدون حكي!» for the high-energy version).

| Line | Use |
| --- | --- |
| «مثّلها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Master tagline |
| «ولّعها… بس بدون حكي!» | Campaign line |
| «سهّرها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Weekend and late-night posts |
| «رمضنها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Ramadan campaign |
| «عيّدها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Eid |
| «اطبخها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Food category promo |
| «اشربها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Partner café nights |
| «احزرها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Challenges and quizzes |

**Rules:** it must be a real verb people say or an obvious playful coinage like «رمضنها». It must make sense for the moment. Use it at most once per post, as the closer.

## Off-limits

We never joke about, or build cards around:
- Politics and political figures
- Religion or religious practice (Ramadan and Eid are celebrated warmly, never mocked)
- Tribes, family names, or someone's origin
- Other dialects, countries or regions as a punchline
- The royal family
- Appearance, disability, gender or class as the joke

When in doubt, cut it.

## Quick checklist before publishing

- [ ] Would jiddo and a 15-year-old both get it?
- [ ] Is it short? Can we cut a line?
- [ ] Is it the right volume for the channel?
- [ ] Is the name spelled ولا كلمة / Wala Kelmeh?
- [ ] Does it need an Arabizi or English version?
- [ ] Is anything on the off-limits list?
