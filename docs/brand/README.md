## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Brand foundation.** Steps 1 to 5 above.

**Phase 2: Core assets**
- Store kit for Google Play, Huawei AppGallery and Samsung Galaxy Store: icon, feature graphic, captioned screenshots, listing copy. iOS comes later.
- Social kit for Instagram and TikTok: post, story and reel templates, profile images, highlight covers
- WhatsApp share cards and a شمّوغ sticker pack

**Phase 3: Marketing**
- Landing page with play-now and install calls to action
- Launch campaign and a 30-day content calendar
- Seasonal campaigns: Ramadan and Eid, weddings, summer, national days
- Café partner kit for «ليلة ولا كلمة»: QR table tent, poster, story template, partner badge
- Sponsor pitch deck for sponsored packs
- Press and creator kit

**Phase 4: Everywhere else**
- Print and merch: physical deck, stickers, posters, شمّوغ plush
- Measurement: UTM links, partner-code tracking, KPIs
