# 3. Logo

Approved by Mikdam on 2026-10-22 after four rounds. Files are in [`brand/logo/`](../../brand/logo/). Earlier rounds are kept in `brand/logo/concepts/` for reference.

## The logo

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Symbol** | A shemagh-red patch with a cream seven-point star (the star from the Jordanian flag) inside a cross-stitched frame |
| **Arabic wordmark** | «ولا كلمة» hand-stitched on a tatreez grid, two stitches thick. **Two flag stars replace the two dots of ة.** |
| **English wordmark** | WALA KELMEH stitched on the same grid, at the same letter height |
| **Taglines** | «مثّلها… بس ولا كلمة!» and *Act it out. Not a word.* |

**One language per lockup.** Arabic and English never share a lockup.

## Lockups

| Lockup | Layout | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arabic horizontal | Symbol on the **right**, wordmark on the left (right to left) | Default for Arabic |
| English horizontal | Symbol on the **left**, wordmark on the right | Default for English |
| Arabic stacked | Symbol, wordmark, «مثّلها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Avatars, posters, splash |
| English stacked | Symbol, wordmark, *Act it out. Not a word.* | Avatars, posters, splash |
| Partner lockup | Our horizontal lockup, a stitched divider, then the partner's logo | Co-branded café and sponsor material |

## Symbol versions

| Version | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary: red patch** | App icon, avatars, the default everywhere |
| Light: cream patch | Light-mode surfaces, and anywhere the background is red |
| Small: star only on red | Anything under 48px: favicon, notifications, tabs |
| One colour, ink or white | Stamps, embroidery, laser engraving, single-colour print |

The star always has one point facing straight up.

## Wordmark colours

| Background | Stitches | Stars on ة |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light (cream) | Ink `#2a1f1a` | Red `#b5121b` |
| Dark (night) | Cream `#f6efe3` | Bright red `#e0293b` |
| Red | Cream `#f6efe3` | Gold `#d9a33a` |
| One colour | All ink or all white | Same as the stitches |

Below 40px letter height, use the **filled-square** version: the same grid with solid squares instead of ✕ stitches.

## Clear space

**x = symbol height ÷ 4.** Keep at least x of empty space on every side of any lockup.

## Minimum sizes

| Element | Screen | Print | Below that |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Symbol with frame | 48px | 12mm | Star-only symbol |
| Star-only symbol | 16px | 5mm | Don't go smaller |
| Stitched wordmark (letter height) | 40px | 12mm | Filled-square version |
| Filled-square wordmark | 16px | 5mm | Symbol alone |
| Horizontal lockup (width) | 200px | 45mm | Symbol alone |

## Don't

- Mix Arabic and English in one lockup
- Recolour the stars (gold is only for red backgrounds)
- Stretch, squash, skew or rotate anything
- Rotate the star (one point always faces up)
- Add shadows, outlines, gradients or other effects
- Put the red patch on a red background (use the cream patch)
- Retype the name in a font. The wordmark is always the stitched artwork.
- Replace the two stars on ة with dots, or with a single star

## Partners and sponsors

- **Partner lockup:** our mark leads (on the right in Arabic, on the left in English), then a vertical stitched divider, then the partner's logo. The partner's logo is never bigger than ours.
- **Partner badge:** a round red badge with a stitched ring and the flag star, reading «شريك ولا كلمة» or WALA KELMEH PARTNER. For café windows, table tents and menus.
- **Sponsored-pack label:** a pill with the red star, reading «مقدّم من {الراعي} · مجاني» or *Presented by {Sponsor} · Free*, with light and dark versions. Every sponsored pack carries it.
