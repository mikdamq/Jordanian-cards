# 1. Brand strategy

Agreed with Mikdam on 2026-10-22. This is the base every later step builds on. Change it here first if the strategy moves.

## Identity direction

**Formalize and refine.** We keep the world the app already lives in: shemagh red, tatreez cross-stitch, cream "fabric" in light mode, "night majlis" in dark mode, and Almarai with Cairo. We add what's missing (a real logo, a mascot, written rules), not a new look.

## Positioning

> **The Jordanian party game that turns any gathering into a sahra.**
> Born in Amman, played by every Arab, wherever they live.

- **Jordanian at heart.** The core deck stays proudly Jordanian. The "only we get this" humour is the hook, and we don't water it down.
- **Open to the region and the world.** Pan-Arab categories (food, movies, animals, actions) work for everyone. Later, dialect packs (فلسطيني، شامي، خليجي، مصري…) give other Arabs their own home deck.
- **Diaspora-friendly.** Arabizi on every card, plus marketing that leans on nostalgia («ريحة البلد») for Jordanians and Arabs abroad.

## Audience

| Segment | What they want | Angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jordanians in Jordan | Family nights, friends' sahras, Ramadan gatherings | Insider jokes, «جمعة العيلة» |
| Diaspora | A piece of home, a game for the kids | Nostalgia, Arabizi, «ريحة البلد» |
| Wider Levant and Arab world | A fun Arabic party game | Shared culture, dialect packs |
| Youth and Gen Z | Chaos, laughs, content to post | TikTok challenges, the شباب pack |
| Kids and families | Easy, safe, cute | شمّوغ, a future «صغار» picture pack |

## Personality

- **Warm** like a majlis
- **Cheeky** like your favourite cousin
- **Proud** of where it comes from, without lecturing

It's never formal, never cringe, and never mocks anyone's dialect or roots.

## Brand promise

> **«اللعبة الأساسية مجانية دايماً، وما في إعلانات بنص اللعب.»**
> *Core deck free forever. Never an ad mid-game.*

Plus: no account needed, and it works offline.

This replaces the old flat "no ads" claim. Sponsors are allowed, but only as **content players choose**, never as interruptions. That is a product rule as well as a brand rule.

## Taglines

| Role | Line | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Master tagline | «مثّلها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Logo lockup, store listings, splash |
| Campaign line | «ولّعها… بس بدون حكي!» | Social, posters, launch, café nights |
| English | *Act it out. Not a word.* | English listings, diaspora |
| Formula | «___ها… بس ولا كلمة!» | Spin-offs: «اعزمها…», «رمضنها…», a partner café's «اشربها… بس ولا كلمة!» |

The brand gesture is a **hand over the mouth, or a finger on the lips 🤫**. It appears in the logo work, in the mascot and in photography.

## Brand architecture: four pillars

ولا كلمة is a sahra brand, and the app is its front door.

1. **🎭 Play.** The app and its free core deck.
2. **📦 Packs.** New and locked categories: sponsored, paid, partner-unlocked and dialect packs.
3. **☕ Places.** Partner cafés and lounges, and «ليلة ولا كلمة» game nights.
4. **👥 People.** Community, creators, challenges, and user-made cards later.

## Monetization principles (future, not yet built)

The core stays free. These are ways to unlock a closed category:

1. **Sponsored pack.** A brand funds a themed category. Players unlock it for free by viewing a short branded intro card.
2. **Partner code.** Scan a QR at a partner café to unlock that venue's own category for the night.
3. **Paid pack.** A one-time purchase for premium sets.
4. **Physical deck.** A QR in the box unlocks a digital bonus pack.

**Café partnerships («ليلة ولا كلمة»), ideas:**
- QR table tents that unlock a venue category about the venue's menu and neighbourhood
- Show a winning score screen to get a discount, a free drink or a free game night
- Monthly tournament nights
- A co-branded kit for each venue: table tent, poster, story template, partner badge

**Rules:** sponsored content is always labelled, always opt-in, and always fits the tone. No brand ever gets to interrupt a turn.

## Mascot

**Direction A: شمّوغ (Shammogh).** A living shemagh: a folded red-and-white shemagh with big expressive eyes, pressing its tassel or a corner over its mouth.

- A **supporting character, never the logo.** It appears in stickers, celebrations, empty states, onboarding, kids content and merch.
- It should work at sticker size and read as cute to kids without feeling childish to adults.

## Channels

- **Stores:** Google Play first, then Huawei AppGallery and Samsung Galaxy Store. iOS later.
- **Social:** Instagram and TikTok
- **Web:** a landing page with play-now and install
- **WhatsApp:** result share cards and stickers
- **Offline:** café partners, posters, and a physical deck later
