# Café partner playbook («ليلة ولا كلمة»)

This is our proposal for how café and restaurant partnerships work. It is a starting point to test with the first real venues, not a contract. It goes with the printable kit (`docs/partner-kit.md`) and the website's cafés page.

**Status:** a draft written by the team. Mikdam reviews it before we talk to the first venue.

## 1. What each side gets

| Who | What they want | What the night gives them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The venue | Fuller tables on quiet nights, longer stays, more orders, content for social | A weekly event that costs no equipment and almost no staff time |
| Guests | A fun reason to go out and play together | A free game they already have on their phone, plus a prize |
| Wala Kelmeh | Players, word of mouth, real-world presence | New players every week, content filmed in real places, and partners to show off |

## 2. Two formats

The app runs a game on **one phone**, with 2 to 4 teams taking turns. The formats are built around that.

### A. «تحدّي الطاولات» (table challenge): the main format

- **Pairing:** two neighbouring tables share one phone, and each table is one team. Bigger tables (6 or more people) can play inside the table instead, split into two teams.
- **Same settings everywhere:** the «تمثيل» mode, 60 seconds a turn, 3 rounds, and the categories the host announces. Fixed settings keep scores fair across pairs.
- **Final:** the winners of each pair go up to a final in front of the room, with one phone and the host holding it up. The final uses the «عالجبين» (heads-up) mode, because it's the most fun to watch.
- **Prize:** the table that wins the final takes the venue's main prize.

### B. «سهرة مفتوحة» (open night): the easy format

For venues that don't want a hosted event.

- Tables play on their own, whenever they like.
- A table that shows staff a final score screen with **10 or more correct answers** gets a small reward (for example, a free tea for the table).
- No host and no schedule. The table tent and table card do all the work.

**Recommendation:** start every new partner on format B for two weeks, then move to format A on one fixed night a week once the staff are comfortable.

## 3. The night, minute by minute (format A, about 90 minutes)

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| −2 days | The venue posts the story «ليلة ولا كلمة» and puts the poster up with the day, time and prize filled in. |
| −30 min | Table tents go on the tables, and the Wi-Fi password goes on the table card (only needed the first time; the game then works offline). |
| 0:00 | The host welcomes everyone and explains the rules in one minute (script below). Each pair of tables scans the QR and sets the agreed settings. |
| 0:05 – 0:45 | Pair games, about 25 to 35 minutes each. The host walks around and helps anyone who's stuck. |
| 0:45 – 0:55 | A break: orders and photos. The host collects the winning table from each pair. |
| 0:55 – 1:15 | The final: «عالجبين» mode, in front of the room. |
| 1:15 | The winners get their prize and a group photo, posted with consent and tagged #ولا_كلمة @walakelmeh. |

### The host's one-minute script (Arabic)

> «أهلاً فيكم بليلة ولا كلمة! كل طاولتين جنب بعض بيلعبوا ضد بعض على جوال واحد. امسحوا الكود عالطاولة، اختاروا «تمثيل»، ٦٠ ثانية، ٣ جولات. واحد بيمثّل، وطاولته بتحزر، وممنوع ولا كلمة! الطاولات الفايزة بتطلع عالنهائي، والجائزة الليلة: ______. يلا!»

## 4. Prizes

The prize comes from the venue. We suggest a ladder, so there's always something to win:

| Level | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Small (format B, or every pair winner) | A free tea or coffee for the table, or a dessert to share |
| Main (the final) | 15–25% off the winning table's bill, or a free shisha or main dish |
| Season (monthly) | A reserved table for next week's night, or a monthly «بطل الشهر» photo on the venue's wall |

**Rule:** a prize never requires a purchase to take part. Anyone at the venue can play.

## 5. Theme nights through the year

These keep a weekly night fresh. Dates follow the Jordanian calendar.

| When | Theme | Idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every Thursday | The standard night | Format A |
| Ramadan | «سهرة سحور» | A late night after taraweeh, with the «رمضان والعيد» category and soft prizes (qatayef for the table) |
| Eid | «عيدية ولا كلمة» | A family afternoon in format B, with a small «عيدية» for the kids' team |
| Exam season end | «خلصنا امتحانات» | A students' night with «باكيت الشباب» and a student discount |
| Football nights | «بين الشوطين» | A 10-minute «عالجبين» round at half-time, with the «رياضة وفن» category |
| End of year | «سهرة آخر السنة» | The year's finalists come back for a champions' night |

## 6. Promotion

**What the venue does:**
- One story two days before, and one on the day, using the template in `brand/social/stories/`.
- The poster on the door and table tents on every table on the night.
- Tags @walakelmeh and #ولا_كلمة in posts from the night.

**What we do:**
- Repost the venue's stories and reels.
- List the venue on the website's partners list (coming once there are 3 partners).
- A «شريك الشهر» (partner of the month) post on our pages.

**Filming and photos:** always ask the table first, and never film kids without their parents' clear permission. If anyone says no, they're left out of the shot.

## 7. Partnership levels (no prices yet)

| Level | The venue commits to | We give |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **صديق** (Friend) | Table tents and the table card on tables | The printable kit, a window sticker, and a listing on the website |
| **شريك** (Partner) | A fixed weekly night in format A, and two stories a month | Everything in Friend, printed for them, plus reposts and a partner-of-the-month spot |
| **شريك مميّز** (Signature), coming soon | Monthly tournaments | A category of cards named after the venue (its menu and neighbourhood), unlocked by the venue's own QR |

Pricing is still open. Our suggestion for the first venues: **everything free for the first 3 to 5 cafés**, in exchange for honest feedback and permission to use photos from the nights.

## 8. A first pilot

- **Who:** 3 cafés in Amman with different crowds (students, families, a late-night shisha place).
- **How long:** 4 weeks, with format B for weeks 1–2 and format A for weeks 3–4.
- **What we measure:**
  - QR scans per venue (each venue gets its own `?ref=` code).
  - Tables that played on the night (the host counts).
  - The venue's own view: did tables stay longer and order more?
  - Story reposts and new followers.
- **What we learn:** whether format A is too much work for staff, which prizes pull people in, and which categories work in public.

## 9. App work this reveals

These are not built yet. They're listed here so they can be planned.

1. **Venue scan counts:** the app should record a `venue/<ref>` event when it opens with `?ref=`, once GoatCounter is set up.
2. **A venue night preset:** a link such as `?ref=venue&preset=night` that opens with the agreed settings, so no one has to set them by hand.
3. **Venue categories:** a partner QR that unlocks a venue's own category (the Signature level).
4. **A shareable result card for the night:** the existing share image, with the venue's name on it.
