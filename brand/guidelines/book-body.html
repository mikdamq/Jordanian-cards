Strategy

Positioning

The Jordanian party game that turns any gathering into a sahra.

Jordanian at heart, with the core deck proudly local. Open to the region and the world through pan-Arab categories, Arabizi on every card, and future dialect packs.

Core deck free forever. Never an ad mid-game. No account needed, and it works offline. Sponsors appear only as labelled, opt-in packs.

02

Voice

The cousin who runs every family sahra: warm, quick, a little cheeky, and everyone feels included.

«___ها… بس ولا كلمة!»

The brand formula: a verb with ها, an ellipsis, then the name. Master tagline «مثّلها… بس ولا كلمة!», campaign line «ولّعها… بس بدون حكي!», and spin-offs like «رمضنها», «عيّدها» and «اشربها».

ArabicArabiziEnglish
مثّلها… بس ولا كلمة!Massilha… bas wala kelmeh!Act it out. Not a word.
ولّعها… بس بدون حكي!Walla3ha… bas bdoon 7aki!Light it up. No talking.
السهرة بتبلّش هونIl sahra btballish honThe sahra starts here.

Tone by channel

  • TikTok, Reels: loud. Full Jordanian, Arabizi, emoji.
  • Instagram, WhatsApp: lively. Jordanian, 1 to 3 emoji.
  • In the app: friendly and short: «جاهزين؟»، «خلص الوقت!»
  • Stores, partners: clear, softer Levantine.
  • Legal, support: calm and plain, no jokes.

Off-limits

  • Politics and political figures
  • Religion or religious practice (Ramadan and Eid are celebrated, never mocked)
  • Tribes, family names, origins
  • Other dialects or regions as a punchline
  • The royal family
  • Appearance, disability, gender or class

Name: ولا كلمة · Wala Kelmeh · handles walakelmeh. Arabizi uses 3 = ع, 7 = ح, 2 = ء, kh, gh, sh. Full guide: docs/brand/02-voice-and-tone.md

Pattern

Four textile devices. Use one or two per layout, never all four.

07

Illustration

Cross-stitch on cream fabric: one X per square, five threads only (red, olive, gold, ink, indigo), grids of about 8 to 12 squares.

08

شمّوغ · Shammogh

A living shemagh: the cloth itself, with a face. A cheeky troublemaker who can’t stop talking, in a game where talking isn’t allowed. No hands: it gestures with its face, its body and the props around it.

Do

  • Keep it beside the logo, never inside it
  • One شمّوغ per screen or post
  • Let it react to the game
  • Shh is always the stitched-shut mouth

Don’t

  • Add hands, arms or fingers
  • Recolour the pattern or use blue
  • Stretch it beyond the built-in moods
  • Give it other brands’ products
